# Practical cabin and 4×4 equipment

Checked 23 October 2026. This is product discovery and a representative-model register, not installation instructions or a stock feed. No seller photographs or CAD were copied into the site. All new preview geometry and fabric maps are authored locally. Actual product revisions, airbag compatibility, anchorage, road legality, stock and shipping must be confirmed with the seller.

## Why these items come first

- An Indian owner reports buying ARMORO console, seat-back and grab-handle storage, plus MOLLE pouches: [Team-BHP owner discussion](https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/official-new-car-reviews/266136-maruti-jimny-review-241.html). This is qualitative demand evidence, not sales ranking or validation of the owner's seat-cover airbag claims. We explicitly do **not** adopt that owner's airbag-risk advice.
- [BigJimny under-seat storage discussion](https://www.bigjimny.com/forum/6-jimny-chat/68886-under-seat-storage) describes first-aid equipment and a spade sliding around and gathering dust. We prioritise restrained storage rather than silently dropping loose tools under seats.
- [Jimny storage discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Jimny/comments/1jd6din) highlights small concealed-storage capacity and the need to stop luggage sliding. [Everyday essentials discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Jimny/comments/1c4nwdk) mentions luggage eyelets, anti-slip liners and folding storage. These are anecdotal ownership reports, not proof of broad popularity.
- [ARB air systems guidance](https://www.arb.com.au/air-systems) explains the useful pairing of inflation, pressure checking and a tyre repair kit. We do not imply a tyre plug can repair sidewalls or replace a serviceable spare.

## Load and placement safeguards

The fuel preview is not a roof-load approval. Fuel can + holder alone weigh 6.22 kg empty; roughly 20 litres of petrol adds around 15 kg, **before the rack**. The Jimny's permitted roof/dynamic/off-road load depends on market, variant, rack and instructions; some limits are very restrictive. Check the actual vehicle manual and rack instructions, weigh the complete loaded arrangement, and do not assume it fits the remaining allowance. Confirm local fuel-container approval, refuelling and transport rules. No spill-safe or crash-safe certification is claimed.

The builder refuses the fuel can without the modelled compatible rack/holder and suppresses it with RTT/traction boards occupying the same space. Water and fuel are different containers and do not share an assumed mount. Optional hardware does not become a rated recovery point. External cans are not attached to the standard rear door in these previews.

All practical subgroups retain a part-specific `finishTarget`, so individual matte/gloss/black edits use the existing shared reversible material layer. Optics and the extinguisher indicator are protected by their semantic materials. Locally owned woven textures/materials dispose with the assembly; shared materials are not disposed. The same builder is used in Garage and Drive.
