# India trip preparation worksheet

Research checked 23 October 2026. This is an editorial preparation worksheet, not live navigation, a fuel-station feed, medical advice, permission to enter a road, or certification of a build.

## What the feature does

The follow-up expedition pass adds three editorial stage groups for every corridor, route-specific packing and vehicle-preparation checklist items, and relevant kit groups. Stage groups are not daily itineraries, turn-by-turn directions or acclimatisation schedules. They use the named route approaches in the official sources below. Packing / servicing prompts are conservative editorial preparation, not mandated modifications, certified equipment specifications or medical instructions. No named fuel station, opening forecast, tyre pressure or safe water depth is invented. Mountain routes receive cold / rain preparation; desert routes receive dust / sun preparation; the coast emphasises wet-weather visibility and brakes. Current notices still need independent checking.

The **Pack & prepare** step now leads with these checklists rather than a long form. Progress for the selected route is stored with the existing local plan and included in text / print exports. Changing route clears old confirmations; unsupported checklist keys are dropped. Fuel/water/load calculations remain available below. Useful kit groups are contextual and optional; a stock Jimny on suitable roads does not automatically require an expedition roof load, snorkel, winch or extra fuel.

- Seven India corridors: Manali–Leh, Srinagar–Leh, Shimla–Kinnaur–Kaza–Manali, Jodhpur–Jaisalmer–Bikaner, Bhuj–Dhordo–Dholavira, Mumbai–Ratnagiri–Goa, and Guwahati–Dirang–Tawang. These cover commonly discussed road-trip directions, not a statistically verified popularity ranking.
- Distance ranges are deliberately broad **editorial planning envelopes**, not mapping measurements. Start/finish scope is stated beside every route. Home approaches, return legs and most detours are excluded unless named.
- Default fuel gaps are **illustrative, unverified planning inputs**, not researched gaps between operating pumps. Users replace and confirm them after checking actual stops. No named station is shown as stocked or open.
- Default 10 km/L is an editable example, not a Jimny economy claim. Enter measured loaded consumption and an explicit reserve. Grades, idling, surfaces and delays can worsen it.
- Fuel calculation: gap / km-per-litre + reserve-litres. Compare against entered main tank plus independently entered extra fuel. An arithmetic balance is not permission or assurance to travel.
- Water: people × user-set litres/person/day × days between confirmed resupply. This is not a physiological recommendation; cooking and delay allowance need personal consideration.
- Ready-to-travel mass is never inferred from a virtual parts list. Users enter a measured mass and exact GVM; axle, roof, mount, tyre and seating limits still need independent checks.
- Suggested months are context, not forecasts. Route/month/fuel/water changes invalidate related old confirmations. A full checklist never changes to “safe” or “travel approved.”
- Local progress uses only the jimnygami-india-trip-v1 browser-storage entry. Corruption/storage denial are surfaced. Text download and browser Print / PDF work without an account.

## Primary references and limitations

### Ladakh corridors

[Leh district: How to Reach](https://leh.nic.in/tourism/how-to-reach/) gives reference lengths of 434 km via Srinagar and 473 km via Manali and broad seasonal context. It still mentions an older Rohtang approach; these are background references, not current turn-by-turn measurements.

[Kargil district: How to Reach](https://kargil.nic.in/how-to-reach/) describes winter access concerns. Its approximate distances do not sum identically to Leh's figures; we do not combine them into a false exact total.

[Leh tourist management portal](https://www.lahdclehpermit.in/) is linked for current tourist fees/permit information. Permissions depend on nationality and area; no blanket ILP requirement is asserted. We do not reproduce medication instructions or a universal acclimatisation schedule.

[J&K Traffic Police](https://trafficpolicejk.in/) is a current-confirmation destination, not scraped live data.

### Spiti

[Himachal Tourism: Spiti Valley](https://himachaltourism.gov.in/destination/spiti-valley/) documents both approaches, protected-area permit considerations for foreign visitors via Kinnaur and limited facilities.

[Kinnaur district: Tourist Guidelines](https://hpkinnaur.nic.in/tourism-guide/) provides protected-area context; current application instructions must be confirmed with the authority.

[Lahaul & Spiti district: Road Status](https://hplahaulspiti.nic.in/road-status/) was reviewed. Its status may be stale or differ from same-day local information; no copied “open” status is persisted. Users must inspect update dates and obtain current confirmation.

The 650–800 km range for this open circuit is an editorial envelope, not a route survey. A closed Manali exit requires a different itinerary and recalculation.

### Rajasthan

[Rajasthan Tourism: Jodhpur](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/jodhpur.html) lists approximately 286 km to Jaisalmer. [Bikaner](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/bikaner.html) lists approximately 331 km to Jaisalmer. [Suggested itineraries](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/suggested-itineraries.html) include this desert-city direction.

Our 600–750 km envelope excludes Jaipur transfers and arbitrary dune excursions. The planner grants no dune, reserve or border access.

### Kachchh

[Kachchh district: White Rann](https://kachchh.nic.in/tourist-place/white-rann-of-kachchh/) explains the salt-marsh environment; this is not justification to drive on it.

[Gujarat Tourism](https://www.gujarattourism.com/) and the [government White Rann permit portal](https://rannpermit.gujarat.gov.in/) are current-confirmation destinations. The direct permit portal timed out in the research tool; its URL is not evidence of portal availability or completed permission. Historic fees and opening hours are not copied.

The 400–600 km loop envelope depends on the exact legal road. No border-side road is approved automatically.

### Konkan

[Goa Department of Tourism: By road](https://goatourism.gov.in/by-road/) documents the coastal approach and ghats. Some highway names/condition language are old; its optimistic road-condition wording is not reproduced as a current promise.

[Maharashtra Tourism](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/) provides destination context. Our 600–800 km envelope includes moderate coastal stops, not an express-route estimate. No live roadworks, driving time, beach or flood access is asserted.

### Tawang

[Tawang district: How to Reach](https://tawang.nic.in/tourism/how-to-reach/) identifies the Guwahati/Bhalukpong/Bomdila/Dirang approach, seasonal risks and permit categories. Reference distances differ across sources and route variants; the 480–600 km envelope is not an assertion that any particular road/tunnel is open.

[Arunachal eILP](https://eilp.arunachal.gov.in/) is the official permit destination. [Tawang tourism guidance](https://tawang.nic.in/tourism/) notes additional permissions for border excursions. Domestic ILP and foreign PAP prompts do not imply eligibility or approval.

### Vehicle, weather and preparation

[NEXA Jimny brochure](https://www.nexaexperience.com/content/dam/msil/nexa/in/en/assets/cars/jimny/documents/NEXA-Jimny-Brochure.pdf) lists 40 L tank capacity, four seats and 1545 kg GVM for its India specification. These are labelled editable defaults. Brochure test mileage is deliberately not used as trip consumption.

[India Meteorological Department](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/) is linked for current warnings. No weather data is fetched or cached by this feature.

[4WD Industry Council: Snatch Strap Guidelines](https://www.4wdcouncil.com.au/resources/snatch-strap-guidelines/) supports warnings about rated recovery points, dangerous stored energy and training. The planner provides no recovery procedure, universal strap rating or claim that an Indian installation is approved by an overseas standard.

Source buttons use the existing data-source-part catalogue ID and close the dialog before the delegated source action. Fitment is not inferred between body styles; overseas sources are not represented as Indian stock.

The kit action dispatches a window event named jimny:trip-kit-request with detail containing routeId, kitId, productIds and categories. Parent integration should open a filtered garage/source selection, never silently apply parts or tick readiness tasks.

## Integration and export

Load assets/trip-planner.css, then assets/trip-planner.js before assets/trip-planner-ui.js. Any button/link with data-open-trip-planner opens it. window.openJimnyTripPlanner() is callable.

Optional build context reads window.getJimnyBuildState() or window.currentPayload() defensively, for selected-part count only. It never modifies that build.

Print uses a same-origin temporary iframe and textContent, not interpolated user HTML. Text exports include assumptions, unchecked tasks, route scope, warnings and source URLs. Downloads occur only on user action.
