## Upholstery and complete touring layout — 22 October 2026

The new door panels and complete touring layout are original custom-build previews informed by available accessories, rather than a single seller's full kit. Research photographs were inspected separately and are not distributed in runtime assets. All fabric colour, roughness and bump maps are original procedural data.

- ARMORO canvas seat covers: https://armoro.com/products/suzuki-jimny-seat-covers — cotton canvas, sand/olive/black options, separate front and rear covers. Used as a material and cover-construction reference; no branding copied. Confirm model/body and airbag compatibility directly.
- DAMD THE GEAR: https://www.damd.co.jp/news/19848/ — houndstooth and practical rear storage. The maker specifies JB64/JB74 front row and explicitly excludes Nomade. Our houndstooth is an original fabric direction available on our model, not a five-door fitment claim for DAMD's cover.
- GRACE upholstered upper door covers: https://compassauto.jp/products/grace-leather-door-trim-panel-upper-covers-jimny-jb74 — inspected upper door wrap, handle wrap and stitching. Our padded centre inserts plus upper/armrest treatment are custom-shaped to the model's door cards. Five-door pieces require a trimmer; the linked reference is JB74.
- EXPLORESETUPS split platform: https://exploresetups.com/products/jimny-xl-5-door-platform — two 555 × 705 mm pieces; deck length with the 460 mm drawer is 1165 mm, width 1110 mm, 15 mm carpeted plywood. Requires the separate false floor and drawers. The model shows this short deck and an original 680 mm-wide passenger extension for sleeping; that extension is not an EXPLORESETUPS SKU.
- EXPLORESETUPS drawer and false floor: links in the table above and https://exploresetups.com/products/jimny-xl-5-door-false-floor . Added an illustrative 300 mm open-drawer state, tray sides and telescopic slides; actual travel and rail load ratings require supplier confirmation.
- EXPED DeepSleep Mat: https://www.exped.com/en/products/sleeping-mats/deepsleep-mat — current MW size is 183 × 65 × 7.5 cm. Our single mat follows those dimensions, with original fabric, valve and pillow. Travel mode shows a rolled mat. No EXPED logo or exact valve/mechanism replica.

The sleeping conversion is five-door only. Rear seat shapes are hidden beneath a representative folded-seat layer; camp mode reclines the original passenger back and hides the passenger headrest and its posts. Driver seat and controls remain intact. The game always uses the packed travel state, irrespective of the saved camp inspection state. An electrical installation, a double berth and a supplier-certified camper conversion are outside this model's scope.
