    What is realistic, and what is simplified?

    Published Jimny wheelbases, tyre size, engine peak torque and automatic gear ratios inform this model. Grip, suspension response, converter behaviour, weight distribution, collisions and terrain are approximations, not measured Jimny dynamics. Difficulty never increases grip. All cars use stock mechanical settings; saved paint, finish, decals and grille can carry over. Performance mods do not.

    This is a game, not driving qualification or a safe-limit calculator. Use your vehicle’s own manual and supervised off-road training. No water crossings, winching, manual-clutch training or certified rollover limits are simulated. Support vehicles are original, unbranded trail companions, not licensed Thar or Wrangler models.

    Control references: Suzuki owner’s manual, pp. 3-19–22 & 3-59–62 · Suzuki specifications. Equipment varies by market and model year.

    Know your controls.

    4-speed automatic only. Use the on-screen pedals or WASD / arrow keys. S and Space brake; they do not select reverse. Choose R while stopped to reverse. Shift + accelerator gives a gentler input. Esc pauses.

    2H
    Rear-wheel drive for normal paved-road driving. Use it for the asphalt course.
    4H
    Four-wheel drive, high range for loose or slippery ground. Straighten the front wheels and avoid shifting while tyres are spinning. Never use 4H or 4L on dry tarmac.
    4L
    Low range for slow technical driving. First select 4H, stop completely, hold the brake, select N, then select 4L. Return to a driving gear before moving. Stop and use N again to leave low range.
    D / 2 / L
    D shifts automatically. 2 limits the gearbox to its lower two gears; L holds first gear in this game. L on the automatic selector is not the same as 4L on the transfer lever.
    Hill descent
    Select 4H or 4L and a forward or reverse gear, below 25 km/h. With the accelerator released, simulated braking targets approximately 10 km/h in 4H or 5 km/h in 4L on a descent. Be ready to brake yourself. It cannot create grip.
    Recover
    A game-only reset to the last gate, costing eight score points. It is not a real vehicle recovery procedure.

    Hills, grip and assistance are simplified. Real systems can behave differently or become unavailable. Follow your own manual; seek qualified instruction. The game does not teach every off-road hazard.
